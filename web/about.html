About

GoGentic is an AI implementation studio for agencies. We build the systems agencies want to sell — AI receptionists, booking automation, custom agent workflows — and deliver them white-label under your brand.

The company is run by Ian Greenberg, an AI researcher at SMU (Southern Methodist University, Dallas). Ian builds and operates production AI agent systems daily: autonomous browsing agents, voice agents, data pipelines processing hundreds of thousands of records, and the infrastructure that makes businesses operable by AI.

Why that matters to your agency: most "AI consultants" are reselling wrappers. We're the people who build the wrappers' insides. The same systems we deploy for clients run our own company's operations — prospecting, outreach, scheduling — so everything we sell is proven on ourselves first.

How we work:

  • Fixed scope, fixed price, defined timeline — no hourly soup
  • Paid pilot first, so you're never betting on slideware
  • We're invisible: your client, your brand, your margin
  • Dallas-based, working with agencies anywhere in the US
